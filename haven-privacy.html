Haven — Privacy Policy

Version 1.9.16 · Effective 29 October 2026

Haven is an app for people who find speaking hard, and for the adults who look after them. This page says, in plain words, what happens to information.

Written by Favour Onilamo (favour187). Contact: favourope78@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no Haven server. Your records are not uploaded to us, because there is nowhere for them to go.
  • Three things leave the phone, and only when you press a button: 1. the words you type into the online AI (Groq), if you add your own key; 2. the words you type into the film search, sent to the Internet Archive; 3. payment details, when you pay for Haven Plus, handled by Paystack.
  • No ads. No analytics. No crash reporting. No tracking. Nothing is sold.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept.

The database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Each install creates its own random key, stored in the app's private preferences on that phone and sent nowhere. Phones that had Haven before version 1.9.16 keep the older key so that no existing record is ever put at risk by an upgrade.

2. What leaves the phone, and when

Only these four, and none of them happen on their own.

a) The online AI (Groq). If — and only if — a caregiver has saved a Groq API key and presses Ask Groq with a working connection, the text of their question is sent to Groq's servers to be answered. No name, no photograph, no record, no list of anything else goes with it. Without a key, Haven never contacts Groq. Groq's own privacy terms apply to that text.

b) The film search (Internet Archive). When a caregiver searches for a film, the words they type are sent to archive.org, which returns public results. The request identifies Haven (its name and project URL) and nothing about you. When a film is downloaded, the file comes from archive.org. Nothing is searched or downloaded unless a caregiver asks.

c) Payments (Paystack, and Haven's payment worker). When someone pays for Haven Plus or Clinic, the email address they type and the payment are handled by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. Haven's small payment worker on Cloudflare receives a payment reference to confirm the purchase, and stores the reference — not your card, not your name. Card details never touch Haven.

d) Sharing between two phones (optional). If a caregiver turns on sharing so another phone receives the day, the summary is sealed on the phone before it leaves and relayed through a mailbox on Cloudflare. The relay stores ciphertext and a sequence number; it cannot read the summary, and the person can turn sharing off at any time, which deletes what is waiting there.

3. What Haven never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDK of any kind.
  • No crash reporting that sends your data anywhere.
  • No selling, renting or sharing personal information.
  • No training of any model on anything the app holds.
  • No background uploads. Nothing is sent while you are not looking.

4. Children

Haven is usually used by or for a child who cannot speak. There is no children's account, no sign-up and no profile on a server. The caregiver sets the app up on the phone and is the person responsible for the information on it. The child's photographs, records and films stay on that device.

6. How long things are kept, and how to delete them

  • Counts of what Haven noticed are kept for 28 days, and at most 3,000 events per person. Older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it: a picture, a record, a film, a child's page. Deleting is permanent.
  • Films are capped at 500 MB per person; the app tells you before it reaches that.
  • Uninstalling Haven deletes the database, the films and the key. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.
  • Removing the caregiver space does not touch the person's own records; a test in the app checks exactly that.

7. Security — honestly

  • The database file is encrypted at rest, with a key unique to that install.
  • The caregiver space is protected by a passphrase of at least 8 characters, stretched with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 rounds); only the salt and the digest are stored. Five wrong attempts lock it for a minute.
  • What encryption cannot do: if the phone is unlocked and in someone else's hands, the app can be opened. Haven protects the file, not a seized, unlocked phone. There is no remote wipe, because there is no server to ask.
  • Because there is no Haven account and no Haven database of users, there is no Haven breach to have.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and under similar laws where you live, you can ask what is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for correction, and ask for deletion. For Haven, the practical answer is the app itself: the information is on your phone, in the app, and you can read, correct or delete all of it there.

For anything held elsewhere — a payment reference held by the payment worker, or an email you sent us — write to favourope78@gmail.com and it will be dealt with.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the version and date at the top change with it, and the new version ships with the next build of the app. The current version is always at this address.

Haven is a communication and record-keeping aid. It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose anything.
